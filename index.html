Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide one-way taxi from Mumbai to Shirdi, Nashik or Lonavala?+
Yes. We run one-way and round-trip outstation cabs from Mumbai to Shirdi, Nashik, Lonavala, Mahabaleshwar and other destinations across Maharashtra, with no return-fare charged on one-way bookings.
How is the fare calculated for outstation trips?+
Fare depends on distance, car type and trip duration. Use the fare calculator above for a quick estimate, then confirm the exact, all-inclusive fare with our team on WhatsApp before you travel.
Can I book a cab for the complete Ashtavinayak Yatra?+
Yes, we plan the full 8-temple Ashtavinayak circuit as a 2-day itinerary from Mumbai or Pune, with a car and driver dedicated to your group for the entire yatra.
Is Swara Travels available for early-morning airport pickups in Mumbai?+
Yes, our booking support is available 24×7, including early-morning and late-night airport pickup and drop within Mumbai.
Which car should I choose for a family or group trip?+
For 2–4 travellers, a sedan like Swift Dzire or Xcent Prime works well. For groups of 5–6, Ertiga, Kia Carens or Chevrolet Enjoy offer more luggage space and comfort.
How do I confirm my booking?+
Fill the booking form above, call us, or message directly on WhatsApp. We confirm your car, driver and fare before the trip begins.
