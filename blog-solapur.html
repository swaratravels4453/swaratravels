Why Pandharpur Matters

Pandharpur, on the banks of the Chandrabhaga (a stretch of the Bhima River) in Solapur district, is often called Maharashtra's spiritual capital. It's the home of Lord Vitthal — a form of Krishna — and the destination of one of India's largest annual walking pilgrimages.

  • Pandharpur — Vitthal-Rukmini Temple and the sacred Chandrabhaga ghat
  • Akkalkot — Swami Samarth Temple, one of Maharashtra's most-visited spiritual sites
  • Tuljapur — Tulja Bhavani Temple, a major Shakti Peeth and Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj's family deity
  • Solapur city — Siddharameshwar Temple, a striking Shiva shrine on an island in Siddheshwar Lake

What Is the Pandharpur Wari?

🚩

The Wari (also called the Vari) is an annual walking pilgrimage to Pandharpur observed for more than 700–800 years, rooted in Maharashtra's Bhakti tradition. Devotees who undertake it are called Warkaris.

The two most revered processions carry a palkhi (palanquin) bearing the sandals of great saints — Sant Dnyaneshwar's palkhi starts from Alandi, and Sant Tukaram's from Dehu, both near Pune. Along the roughly 250–300 km, three-week walk, many smaller palkhis join in, culminating at the Vitthal Rukmini Temple on Ashadhi Ekadashi.

4 Must-Visit Sites in the Solapur Pilgrimage Circuit

These four sites form Swara Travels' most-booked Solapur package, comfortably covered in a 2-day trip.

Pandharpur

Vitthal Rukmini Temple — Pandharpur

📍 Pandharpur · 75 km from Solapur · 340 km from Mumbai

Pandharpur is the spiritual capital of Maharashtra — home of Lord Vitthal (Krishna). Millions of devotees take the Wari pilgrimage here for Ashadhi and Kartiki Ekadashi. The Chandrabhaga ghat is sacred for ancestor rituals and ritual bathing.

✨ Why Famous: Spiritual capital of Maharashtra · Chandrabhaga holy ghat

Akkalkot

Swami Samarth Temple — Akkalkot

📍 Akkalkot · 40 km from Solapur

Dedicated to Shri Swami Samarth Maharaj, believed by devotees to be an incarnation of Lord Dattatreya. Swami lived in Akkalkot for many years, and the site remains one of Maharashtra's most-visited spiritual destinations.

✨ Why Famous: Incarnation of Lord Dattatreya · Major devotee following
📲 Book Cab for This Temple
Tuljapur

Tulja Bhavani Temple — Tuljapur

📍 Tuljapur · 45 km from Solapur

One of the 51 sacred Shakti Peethas of India, and the kuladevata (family deity) of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, who is said to have sought her blessings before battles. Thousands of devotees visit especially during Navratri and Diwali.

✨ Why Famous: Shivaji Maharaj's kuladevata · Major Navratri festival
📲 Book Cab for This Temple
Siddharameshwar

Siddharameshwar Temple — Solapur

📍 Solapur City · Siddheshwar Lake

A Lord Shiva temple built on an island in Siddheshwar Lake — one of the most scenic temple settings in Maharashtra, founded by Saint Siddharama. It hosts a large Mahashivratri fair every year and makes a peaceful final stop in Solapur city.

✨ Why Famous: Island temple on a lake · Grand Mahashivratri fair
