Places to Visit in Solapur District

Four of the region's most-visited spiritual sites, all reachable in one planned trip.

Akkalkot

Swami Samarth Temple — Akkalkot

📍 Akkalkot, Solapur District · 40 km from Solapur

Dedicated to Shri Swami Samarth Maharaj, believed to be an incarnation of Lord Dattatreya, this temple draws devotees who believe a visit here helps fulfil wishes and ease life's difficulties. Swami Samarth lived in Akkalkot for many years, and the town remains closely tied to his legacy.

✨ Why Famous: Legacy of Swami Samarth Maharaj · Large daily devotee footfall · Peaceful temple town atmosphere

Pandharpur

Vitthal-Rukmini Temple — Pandharpur

📍 Pandharpur · 75 km from Solapur

Pandharpur is one of Maharashtra's most important pilgrimage sites, dedicated to Lord Vitthal (a form of Krishna) and Goddess Rukmini. Lakhs of devotees, known as Warkaris, walk here every year for the Ashadhi Ekadashi Wari — one of India's largest annual pilgrimages.

✨ Why Famous: Spiritual capital of Maharashtra · Ashadhi Ekadashi Wari pilgrimage · Centuries-old Bhakti tradition

Solapur City

Siddheshwar Temple

📍 Solapur City · Solapur City Centre

Built by Saint Siddharama and dedicated to Lord Shiva, this temple sits on an island in the middle of Siddheshwar Lake right within Solapur city. Its calm, water-surrounded setting makes it a peaceful stop, and it's especially lively during the annual Mahashivratri fair.

✨ Why Famous: Unique lake-island setting · Mahashivratri fair · Easy city-centre stop
📲 Book Cab for This Temple
Tuljapur

Shree Tulja Bhavani Temple — Tuljapur

📍 Tuljapur · 45 km from Solapur

Tulja Bhavani is one of Maharashtra's most revered Shakti Peethas and holds special significance as the family deity (Kuldevata) of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. The temple draws especially large crowds during Navratri and Diwali, when the town takes on a festive, celebratory atmosphere.

✨ Why Famous: Kuldevata of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj · Important Shakti Peetha · Major Navratri celebrations
