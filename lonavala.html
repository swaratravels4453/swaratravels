What to Visit

Places to Visit in & Around Lonavala

Waterfalls in monsoon, caves year-round, and viewpoints that make the drive worth it.

Monsoon Favourite

Bhushi Dam

📍 Lonavala · 8 km from town centre

The stepped spillway at Bhushi Dam is Lonavala's best-known monsoon spot, where families and groups wade in the flowing water with the Sahyadri hills as a backdrop. It's busiest on weekends between July and September.

✨ Best Time: July–September (monsoon) · Carry a change of clothes · Popular for family day trips

Heritage Site

Karla & Bhaja Caves

📍 Karla / Bhaja · ~11 km from Lonavala

These ancient rock-cut Buddhist caves date back over 2,000 years. Karla Caves has one of India's largest and best-preserved chaitya halls, while Bhaja, a short drive away, is known for its simpler viharas and nearby waterfall in the monsoon.

✨ Why Visit: Among India's oldest rock-cut caves · Short, easy climb · Great combo with a Lonavala day trip

Viewpoint

Tiger's Point (Tiger Leap)

📍 Lonavala · 8 km from town centre

Named for its sheer cliff edge that looks like a tiger's leap, this viewpoint offers a sweeping view over the valley below and is especially dramatic when the monsoon clouds roll in at eye level.

✨ Best Time: Early morning or sunset · Best visited with Lion's Point nearby · Can get slippery in heavy rain
📲 Book Cab for This Trip
For Trekkers

Rajmachi Point & Fort

📍 Rajmachi · ~15 km from Lonavala

Rajmachi Point offers one of the best valley views near Lonavala, and doubles as the starting point for the popular Rajmachi Fort trek a good half-day add-on for travellers who want more than just sightseeing.

✨ Why Visit: Panoramic valley views · Starting point for the Rajmachi trek · Quieter than the main Lonavala viewpoints
