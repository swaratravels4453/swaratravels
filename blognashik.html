Why Visit Nashik?

Nashik is often called Maharashtra's "Wine Capital" — and it's also one of the four cities in India that hosts the Kumbh Mela. It's the only place where a single trip can cover a Jyotirlinga darshan, a Ramayana-era township, and a vineyard tasting room, all within a 20 km radius on the banks of the sacred Godavari river.

  • Pilgrims: Trimbakeshwar (one of the 12 Jyotirlingas), Panchvati (where Ram, Sita and Lakshman lived during exile), Saptashrungi (a major Shakti Peeth) and Shirdi (90 km away).
  • Kumbh Mela pilgrims: Nashik-Trimbakeshwar hosts the Simhastha Kumbh Mela roughly once every 12 years, and the next edition begins in late 2026.
  • Weekend travellers: Sula Vineyards, Pandavleni Caves, Anjaneri Hills and the Godavari ghats make Nashik a genuine 1–2 day leisure break, not just a pilgrimage stop.

Places to Visit in Nashik

These ten stops cover Nashik's temples, history and wine country — think of it as your yatra path.

🛕 Spiritual & Religious Places

🛕

1. Trimbakeshwar Temple

One of the 12 Jyotirlingas in India and genuinely one-of-a-kind — the Shivalinga here has three faces representing Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva. It's also considered the source of the Godavari River and a major site for Kaal Sarp Dosha puja and Kumbh Mela rituals.

✨ Why Famous: Only three-faced Jyotirlinga in India · Source of the Godavari · Major Kumbh Mela site

🌿

2. Panchvati & Ramkund

Panchvati is where Ram, Sita and Lakshman are believed to have lived during their 14 years of exile. Right beside it, Ramkund is a sacred ghat on the Godavari — devotees immerse ancestral ashes here believing it grants moksha.

✨ Why Famous: Ramayana exile site · Ramkund gives moksha to departed souls
📲 Book Cab for This Place
🪨

3. Sita Gufa (Sita's Cave)

An ancient natural cave in Panchvati believed to be where Goddess Sita stayed during her time in Nashik. Inside is a small temple with idols of Ram, Sita and Lakshman, reached through a narrow passage — a memorable experience for devotees.

✨ Why Famous: Cave linked to Sita's exile · Ram-Sita-Lakshman shrine inside
📲 Book Cab for This Place
🖤

4. Kalaram Temple

A 270-year-old temple built around a rare black-stone Ram idol. It's also historically significant as the site of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar's 1930 satyagraha for temple entry rights — a landmark in India's social reform movement.

✨ Why Famous: Rare black-stone Ram idol · Ambedkar's 1930 satyagraha site
📲 Book Cab for This Place
🏛️

5. Muktidham Mandir

Built entirely in pure white Rajasthani marble, this temple has the complete Bhagavad Geeta carved onto its walls and replicas of all 12 Jyotirlingas in one place — great for photography.

✨ Why Famous: Full Gita carved on marble · All 12 Jyotirlingas replicated
📲 Book Cab for This Place
⛰️

6. Saptashrungi Devi

A powerful Shakti Peeth perched at around 4,600 ft, with an 8-foot natural rock idol carved with 18 hands. Reaching the sanctum means climbing roughly 470 steps (~45 min), or take the ropeway. Sahyadri views from the top are stunning.

✨ Why Famous: Major Shakti Peeth · 18-armed rock idol · Ropeway available
📲 Book Cab for This Place

🌿 Nature & Adventure

🏔️

7. Pandavleni Caves

24 ancient Buddhist caves dating roughly 250 BC to 600 AD, with detailed carvings and stupas. A short hike rewards you with a panoramic view over Nashik city from the hilltop.

✨ Why Famous: 250 BC Buddhist caves · Panoramic city view
📲 Book Cab for This Place
🐒

8. Anjaneri Hills

Believed to be the birthplace of Lord Hanuman, this hill about 25 km from Nashik is both a pilgrimage and trekking destination. The 2–3 hour trek passes through forest to a summit temple of Anjani Mata, with sweeping Sahyadri views.

✨ Why Famous: Birthplace of Lord Hanuman · Popular trekking hill
📲 Book Cab for This Place

🍷 Modern & Leisure Attractions

🍇

9. Sula Vineyards

India's most famous winery and the reason Nashik is called the "Wine Capital of India." Enjoy vineyard tours, tastings and a lake-view resort — catch the annual SulaFest if you're visiting in February.

✨ Why Famous: India's #1 winery · Annual SulaFest
📲 Book Cab for This Place
🌊

10. Godavari Ghat

The sacred riverfront of the Godavari, lined with ancient temples, best experienced at the daily morning or evening aarti. This is also the main bathing ghat during the Kumbh Mela, held once every 12 years.

✨ Why Famous: Daily aarti · Main Kumbh Mela bathing ghat

Nashik + Shirdi Combo

🪔

Many travellers extend their Nashik trip to Shirdi — home to the Samadhi Mandir of Sai Baba, one of India's most-visited pilgrimage sites. Nashik to Shirdi is about 90 km, roughly 2 hours by road via Sinnar and Kopargaon.

Inside the complex you'll also find the Dwarkamai (where Baba lived for 60+ years), the Chavadi, and the Lendi Baug garden. Daily rituals run roughly 5 AM to 10 PM, with the temple open through the night on select festivals.

FAQs — Nashik Tour

What is the cab fare from Mumbai to Nashik?

Fixed outstation fare — no surge, unlike Uber/Ola. Sedan (Dzire/Xcent) typically starts around ₹12–14/km, 6-seater SUV around ₹16–18/km. WhatsApp 8104050420 for the exact quote on your date.

How far is Nashik from Mumbai and Pune?

Nashik is about 165 km from Mumbai (~3.5 hours via NH-3) and about 210 km from Pune (~4 hours). Both are comfortable one-day or overnight drives.

Can I visit Nashik and Shirdi together in one trip?

Yes! Nashik to Shirdi is about 90 km (~2 hours). Day 1: Nashik darshan, Day 2: Shirdi Sai Baba. Same cab and driver throughout — WhatsApp for the combined package rate.

When is the Nashik-Trimbakeshwar Kumbh Mela 2027?

The Dhwajarohan (flag-hoisting) is on 31 October 2026, marking the official start. Main Shahi Snan dates are expected around 2 August, 31 August and 11–12 September 2027, with the Mela continuing into mid-2028. Reconfirm exact dates closer to the time.

Is the climb to Saptashrungi Devi Temple difficult?

It's a moderate climb — roughly 470 steps, about 45 minutes to an hour. A ropeway is available near the base for elderly visitors or anyone who'd rather skip the stairs.

Can Nashik be covered in one day from Mumbai?

Yes — starting by 5–6 AM you can cover Trimbakeshwar, Panchvati, Ramkund, Kalaram and Muktidham comfortably in a day. For Saptashrungi or a Shirdi extension, an overnight stay is recommended.

Will cabs be available in Nashik during Kumbh Mela 2027?

Yes, but demand and traffic restrictions rise sharply near the Shahi Snan dates. We recommend booking a few months ahead for Kumbh-period travel to lock in a fixed fare before availability tightens.
