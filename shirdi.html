Places to Visit in & Around Shirdi

Plan your darshan and add these nearby stops to your itinerary.

Main Shrine

Shri Sai Baba Samadhi Mandir

📍 Shirdi, Ahmednagar District · Town Centre

The Samadhi Mandir houses the resting place of Shri Sai Baba and is the heart of every visit to Shirdi. The marble idol of Sai Baba sits directly above the Samadhi, and the temple complex remains open through the day for darshan, aarti and prasad distribution.

✨ Plan for: Early-morning Kakad Aarti · Special darshan lines on Thursdays & festival days · On-site prasad counter

Historic Site

Dwarkamai

📍 Adjacent to Samadhi Mandir · 5 min walk

This old mosque-like structure was where Sai Baba lived for much of his life, kept a sacred fire (dhuni) burning continuously, and gave darshan to devotees. The dhuni's ash (udi) is still distributed to visitors as a blessing.

✨ Why Visit: Sai Baba's actual residence · Sacred dhuni still maintained · A quieter, reflective stop right beside the main temple
📲 Book Cab for This Trip
Garden

Lendi Baug

📍 Shirdi Town · 10 min from Samadhi Mandir

A peaceful garden personally tended by Sai Baba, home to a Nandadeep (eternal lamp) that has reportedly stayed lit since his time. It's a calm, shaded stop for families travelling with elders or young children after darshan.

✨ Why Visit: Quiet, green break from temple crowds · Historic Nandadeep lamp · Easy walking distance from the main complex

Popular Combo

Shani Shingnapur

📍 Shani Shingnapur · ~70 km from Shirdi

Famous for its open-air Shani idol and the tradition of homes here having no doors, Shani Shingnapur is the most-requested add-on for travellers already coming this far from Mumbai. Most groups cover both towns comfortably within a well-planned two-day trip.

✨ Why Combine: Same direction as Shirdi · Doubles the trip's value in one outstation booking · Best done as a 2-day itinerary with an overnight stay
